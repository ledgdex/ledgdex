LEDGDEX HAS MOVED


Ledgdex has moved to https://ledgdex.github.io: the viewer, live markets and docs.

The new address is an origin of its own, so no other site can read what the viewer keeps in your browser. This address is shared by every matrixdex site.

If you kept a key or your ledger in the viewer here, move them: open the old viewer, unlock your key, reveal any sealed bid you still have to reveal, then use Download key backup and Download ledgdex.jsonl. In the new viewer, paste the "secret" from the backup file into the key box, choose Use this key, then Keep, sealed with your passphrase, and open your ledgdex.jsonl as your ledger. Then use Forget key in the old viewer.
