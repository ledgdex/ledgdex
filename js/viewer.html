Ledgdex viewer

Load a ledger, check every signature and hash in your browser, and sign messages to it. Keep your own ledger here too: copies of what you send and the receipts sellers give you. Nothing is uploaded: keys stay in this browser, and ledgers and messages are files you download.

Your key

Kept in this browser's storage and never sent anywhere. Messages to other ledgers are signed with a self's owner key, so use the key your own ledger names as its owner.

Public key: none

Your ledger

Optional, and needed for triple entry: every message you sign is kept here as "sent", and the seller's records of them as receipts. Download it and publish it in your dex (as ledgdex.jsonl), where sellers collect what you sent.
