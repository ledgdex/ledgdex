SEALED-BID AUCTIONS


An auction takes secret bids until close. A bid signs only the hash of the amount and a random nonce, so nobody, not even the seller, sees it; after the close each bidder reveals; once reveal_until passes, the best valid reveal wins, by rule.

{"item": {"title": "Map of Bombay, 1893"}, "currency": "INR",
 "close": "2026-11-01T12:00:00Z", "reveal_until": "2026-11-02T12:00:00Z",
 "best": "highest", "reserve": 500000}

1. ledgdex auction gallery auction.json

2. Bidders bid (the amount and nonce stay in ~/.ledgdex/bids until the reveal): 

ledgdex bid me https://gallery.example AUCTION_ID --amount 650000

3. After close: 

ledgdex reveal me https://gallery.example AUCTION_ID

4. The seller records bids and reveals as they come (ledgdex record). After reveal_until, the state names the winner (awarded); the trade goes on as a claim: paid, delivered, confirmed.

Rules: one bid per key; bids after close and reveals outside the window are ignored; a reveal must match its bid; "best": "lowest" runs a procurement (the lowest offer at or under reserve wins); ties go to the earliest bid. A ledger's state is judged at its last entry, so a seller announces the result with any entry after reveal_until (a note). Example: Auction.
