BUYING


A buyer signs claims, payments and confirmations with its own key and keeps a copy of each as sent in its own ledger: publishing your dex is sending them. You need your own ledgdex to buy (ledgdex init me --name "Asha" --key asha), or only a browser (The Viewer).

1. Find the offer: on the seller's dex, or through an index (Indexes and Discovery).

2. Claim it. ledgdex reads the seller's ledger, checks the offer is open, and signs the offer's exact hash and price: 

ledgdex claim me https://farm.example OFFER_ID --quantity 2

3. Send it: publish your dex (ledgdex publish me), and the seller collects it with ledgdex record shop --from https://asha.example; or send the file claim-....json any way you like.

4. Keep the receipts: the seller's signed records of your messages. 

ledgdex receipt me https://farm.example

5. Pay as the offer says (outside the ledger), then tell the seller, and confirm once the goods arrive: 

ledgdex pay me https://farm.example CLAIM_ID --method upi --ref UTR123
ledgdex confirm me https://farm.example CLAIM_ID

6. If something goes wrong: Disputes and Arbiters.

Your receipts are your proof: an entry signed by the seller saying it recorded your claim at that position of its ledger. If the seller ever rewrites its ledger, ledgdex check on your ledger shows it (Checking for Tampering).
