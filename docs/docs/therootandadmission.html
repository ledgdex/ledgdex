THE ROOT AND ADMISSION


The root is a ledger whose admit and revoke entries say who is in a market, whose recover entries name new keys for lost ones, and whose owner is the default arbiter. A dex names it once: ledgdex init ... --root https://market.example (or "ledgdex": {"root": ...} in config.json).

The root is pinned by its ledger id (root_id, written the first time it is read): another ledger at that address, or a broken one, is refused. The last copy read is kept (.ledgdex-root.jsonl), so the market keeps working when the root's site is down, and an older copy is never taken.

"allow": "admitted" means admitted by the seller's own ledger and by the root, at the time the claim was made: a later revoke never undoes a past sale. Admit and revoke: 

ledgdex admit market ed25519:KEY --name "Asha" --note "member since 2026"
ledgdex revoke market ed25519:KEY --reason "left the market"

A root can be a market's, a city's or The Matrix's. Running one: the README's Running the root.
