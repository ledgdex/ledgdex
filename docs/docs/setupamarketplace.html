SET UP A MARKETPLACE


A market for a place, a trade or a community, with no platform in the middle: every member keeps their own ledgdex, and one operator keeps a ledger that is both the index (which shops are listed) and the root (who is admitted). Shops sell to admitted members; buyers find offers through the index; the operator is the default arbiter of disputes. Example: Marketplace does all of this on one machine.

1. The operator makes the market's ledgdex and publishes it (Publishing a Ledgdex Online): 

ledgdex init market --name "Ratnagiri Market" --about "Fruit sellers of Ratnagiri" --key market \
    --url https://market.example --dest git@github.com:YOU/market.git --branch main
ledgdex publish market

2. Each shop makes its own ledgdex under the market: 

ledgdex init farm --name "Mango Farm" --key farm --root https://market.example

3. Shops and buyers send the operator their public key (printed by ledgdex keygen NAME or ledgdex init). The operator checks who they are, off the ledger, and admits them: 

ledgdex admit market ed25519:SHOP_KEY --name "Mango Farm"
ledgdex admit market ed25519:BUYER_KEY --name "Asha"
ledgdex publish market

4. The operator lists the shops: 

ledgdex list market https://farm.example --note "mangoes"

5. Shops offer to admitted members only ("allow": "admitted"; a shop admits the buyers it serves too, with ledgdex admit farm KEY), or to anyone ("any").

6. Buyers read the index to find offers: 

ledgdex discover https://market.example --offers

7. Everyone runs ledgdex check on the ledgers they trade with, daily (Checking for Tampering): any rewrite is caught, with signed proof.

What the operator can and cannot do: it decides membership and rules disputes (signed, in public); it cannot change anyone's ledger, hide a sale, or set a price. Its key is the most valuable one in the market: keep it offline and use a device key day to day (Keys, Devices and Recovery).

Variations: without --root, a market is just an index anyone can be listed in. Several markets can list the same shop. A shop under no root arbitrates its own offers unless it names an arbiter (Disputes and Arbiters).
