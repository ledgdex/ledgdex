LEDGDEX DEX


About (Ledgdex is a simple market on a dex. Every self keeps a signed, append-only ledger in its own dex, and a trade exists three times: the buyer's signed claim, the seller's signed record of it, and the buyer's copy of that record.)

Viewer (Load any ledgdex ledger and check every signature and hash in your browser. Keep your own ledger here too, sign messages to sellers, collect their receipts, and download your ledger as a complete dex. Nothing is uploaded: your key stays in this browser.)
