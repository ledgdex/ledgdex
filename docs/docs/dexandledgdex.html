DEX AND LEDGDEX


The dex is the core. A dex is a website dexweb builds from a folder: data.json (its pages), config.json (its look and where it is published), to_add/ (files to turn into pages) and gen/ (the site). A dex works on its own, is freely shared, is readable by people and bots, and is hosted anywhere static files are (dex hosting).

Dex + ledger = ledgdex. A ledgdex is a dex with one more file, ledgdex.jsonl: a ledger signed by its owner, entry by entry, each entry chained to the one before by its hash. ledgdex reads the ledger, replays it into a state (offers, claims, payments, disputes, auctions, admissions, listings) and writes pages for it into the same data.json, next to your own pages; dexweb builds the site as always. Nothing else changes: same folder, same build, same hosting.

dex          = data.json + config.json + to_add/        ->  gen/ (website)
ledgdex      = dex + ledgdex.jsonl (signed ledger)      ->  gen/ (website + ledger pages + ledger)
a market     = many ledgdexes that read each other's ledgers

So everything you know about dexes holds: dexweb's getting started, its styles and themes, publishing with python run.py. ledgdex adds the commands that sign and record (Command Line Options), and refuses to publish a ledger that does not extend the one already published ("append_only": ["ledgdex.jsonl"] in config.json).

Which to use: a dex when you publish (documents, a shop window, a journal); a ledgdex when what you publish must be trusted later: offers people buy from, records of who bought what, admissions to a market. A dex can become a ledgdex at any time: ledgdex init on an existing dex folder keeps its pages and config.json and adds a ledger.

This documentation is both: a dex (the written pages) and a ledgdex (Ledgdex Docs ledger, Example offer: Alphonso mangoes and Example auction: Map of Bombay, 1893 are generated from its ledger). Load that ledger in the viewer, or check it: 

ledgdex verify https://matrixdex.github.io/ledgdex/docs
