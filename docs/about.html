ABOUT


Ledgdex is a simple market on a dex. Every self keeps a signed, append-only ledger in its own dex, and a trade exists three times: the buyer's signed claim, the seller's signed record of it, and the buyer's copy of that record.

The viewer verifies ledgers and keeps yours, in this browser. Its own pages, and the dex it downloads for you, are built the way dexweb builds them.
