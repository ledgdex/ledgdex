GETTING STARTED


Ledgdex is a simple market on a dex. A dex is a website made by dexweb from your own files; a ledgdex is a dex with a signed, append-only ledger in it (ledgdex.jsonl). Every seller and buyer keeps one. A trade then exists three times: the buyer's signed claim, the seller's signed record of it, and the buyer's copy of that record. No server, no blockchain: SHA-256, Ed25519 and plain files. Dex is the core; dex + ledger is a ledgdex.

These docs are a ledgdex too: its own ledger (signed once, as a demonstration) is rendered into the pages listed here, next to the written ones.

1. Install ledgdex (Python 3.8 or later, with git; more ways in Installation): 

pip install "ledgdex @ git+https://github.com/matrixdex/ledgdex@v1.0.16#subdirectory=py"

2. Make your ledgdex, a dex with a new ledger and a key kept in ~/.ledgdex: 

ledgdex init shop --name "Mango Farm" --key farm

3. Offer something. Prices are integers in the smallest unit of the currency (120000 is INR 1,200.00): 

cat > offer.json <<EOF
{"item": {"title": "Alphonso mangoes"}, "quantity": 10, "unit": "dozen",
 "currency": "INR", "price": 120000, "pay": [{"method": "upi", "to": "farm@bank"}]}
EOF
ledgdex offer shop offer.json

4. Open shop/gen/index.html: your dex, with a page for your ledger and one for the offer. Your own pages go in shop/data.json or shop/to_add/ as in any dex.

5. Put it online (Publishing a Ledgdex Online), and buyers claim from it (Buying). Example: First Trade runs a whole trade on one machine.

Where to go next: simple users setting up a market start at Set Up a Marketplace; developers at Extending Ledgdex and the API Reference; bots and agents at Bots and Agents. Every command is in Command Line Options, every example in Examples.
