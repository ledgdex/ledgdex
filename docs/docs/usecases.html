USE CASES


Where a ledgdex fits: trades between people or machines who keep their own records and want them to be evidence.

farm or shop selling to regulars        offers, claims, payment notices, receipts
a market of local sellers              a root (admission) + an index (discovery) + shops
freelance services                     an offer per service, delivered with a link, disputes to a guild
digital goods and licences             a seller bot delivering on payment
art, maps, collectables                sealed-bid auctions, with a reserve
procurement and tenders                "best": "lowest" auctions: the lowest bid under the budget wins
robots and machines selling service    device keys, per-unit claims, daily settlement
a club or co-operative                 admission through a root, members-only offers
public records of supply               notes and offers as dated, signed statements

Where it does not: anything needing a shared global order or instant finality between strangers (ledgdex has no consensus, by design), or money itself: payment happens outside, and the ledger records it.
