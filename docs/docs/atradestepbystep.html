A TRADE, STEP BY STEP


Who signs what, in which ledger, and the claim's status after each step. Example: First Trade runs it.

step                         signed by   kept in                     claim status
offer                        seller      seller ledger               (offer open)
claim                        buyer       buyer (sent), seller        accepted, or rejected with a reason
receipt of the claim         buyer       buyer ledger                (the third entry)
paid                         buyer       buyer (sent), seller        accepted, paid
received                     seller      seller ledger               accepted, received
delivered                    seller      seller ledger               accepted, delivered
confirmed                    buyer       buyer (sent), seller        closed (once received + delivered + confirmed)
dispute                      either      seller ledger               disputed
ruling                       arbiter     seller ledger               released, refunded or split

Rejection reasons, in the order they are tested: unknown_offer, offer_changed, withdrawn, expired, self_claim, not_allowed, bad_quantity, price_mismatch. Messages that break no structure but no rule accepts (a payment from someone else, a ruling by the wrong key, a message recorded twice) are kept and listed in the state's ignored with a reason; they never break the ledger.

Read the state of any ledger: 

ledgdex state https://farm.example
ledgdex state shop | python3 -m json.tool
