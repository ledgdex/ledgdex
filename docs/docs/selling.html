SELLING


A seller signs offers into its own ledger, records the claims that come in, and records payment and delivery.

1. Write the offer as JSON. Required: item.title, quantity, currency, price. The rest has defaults: unit "unit", allow "any", pay [], terms "", item.text "", item.media [], and the arbiter is the root's owner, or the seller when the dex names no root. 

{"item": {"title": "Alphonso mangoes", "text": "One dozen, ripe.",
          "media": [{"url": "https://farm.example/mango.jpg", "hash": "sha256:..."}]},
 "quantity": 10, "unit": "dozen", "currency": "INR", "price": 120000,
 "allow": "any", "pay": [{"method": "upi", "to": "farm@bank"}],
 "terms": "Delivered in Ratnagiri within 2 days.", "expires": "2026-12-31T23:59:59Z"}

2. Sign and record it: 

ledgdex offer shop offer.json

3. Collect claims: from each buyer's published dex, or from files buyers send you. 

ledgdex record shop --from https://asha.example --from https://ravi.example
ledgdex record shop claim-1a2b3c4d5e6f.json

4. When the money arrives, and when you deliver: 

ledgdex received shop CLAIM_ID 240000
ledgdex delivered shop CLAIM_ID --note "by courier"

5. Stop selling: 

ledgdex withdraw shop OFFER_ID

6. Publish after each step others need to see: 

ledgdex publish shop

Rules the state enforces (anyone recomputes them): a claim must name the offer by id and by hash (the offer cannot change under it), pay the offer's price, ask for 1 to the remaining quantity, come from someone allow admits, arrive before expires, and not come from the seller itself; otherwise it is kept and marked rejected with the reason. Two claims for the last unit: the first recorded wins.

Media are linked, never embedded: a URL and the SHA-256 of the file, so a picture cannot change after a sale without ledgdex check --media noticing.

Prices are integers in the smallest unit: INR and USD in paise and cents (2 digits), JPY in yen, BTC in satoshi. Any currency code works; pages format the ones they know. See A Trade, Step by Step and Example: First Trade.
