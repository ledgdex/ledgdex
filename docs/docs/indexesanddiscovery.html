INDEXES AND DISCOVERY


An index is a ledger that lists other ledgers: list and delist entries with the ledger's id, address, owner key and a note. Anyone can run one; a market's index is usually its root.

ledgdex list market https://farm.example --note "mangoes"
ledgdex delist market LEDGER_ID --reason closed

Discovery reads an index, and with --offers every listed ledger's open offers and auctions, with their signed price and terms (and --json for programs): 

ledgdex discover https://market.example --offers
ledgdex discover https://market.example --offers --json

A listed address that serves another ledger than the one listed is reported, not trusted. Example: Marketplace and Example: Bots choose offers this way.
